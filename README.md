## 📖 How to Use AutoMacro

### Step 1: Open the Application
After installation, launch AutoMacro. You will see a small floating toolbar on your screen.

### Step 2: Start Recording
Click the red "Record" button or press the default hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+R). The toolbar will turn red to indicate recording is active.

### Step 3: Perform Your Actions
Move your mouse, click buttons, type text, or do anything you want to automate. AutoMacro tracks every input.

### Step 4: Stop Recording
Press the same hotkey again or click the "Stop" button. Your recording is now saved as a script.

### Step 5: Play Back the Script
Click the green "Play" button or press the play hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+P). Watch as AutoMacro repeats your exact actions.

### Step 6: Adjust Settings
Use the settings menu to change hotkeys, adjust playback speed, set loop counts, or edit scripts.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

- **Recording doesn't start** – make sure no other application is using the hotkey
- **Playback is too fast/slow** – adjust speed in settings
- **Actions don't match** – ensure the application window is in the same position as when recorded
- **Script won't save** – check disk space and folder permissions
- **Antivirus warning** – add AutoMacro to your antivirus exceptions list (it's safe)
